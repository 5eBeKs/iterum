# Defect zoo

Every defect this project knows, by what a client would get wrong: which layer stops it,
what that layer charged, and what nothing stops. Generated by `python bench.py zoo`;
do not edit by hand.

- Machine corpus: `examples/mutations` of MAX v2 at `a3eb90f` -- declarations its own suite holds true there.
- Reviewer corpus: this bench at `8b51d7e`; launches recorded over MAX v2 revisions `05bbc25`, `06eb683`, `0703cb4`, `1d34cf0`, `2a6f8dc`, `2cc51dd`, `7e1d71f`, `920704d`, `99dbc4b`, `a3eb90f`, `b409142`, `b7adc4a`, `bc6f71b`, `d6a41ff`, `e48d886`, `fa38876`.
- Written 2026-09-19.

Three layers, in the order a defect meets them: the **machine** gates (free, deterministic,
every run), the stage's **auditor** (one model launch per stage), and the final **red team**
(one launch per run, after the auditor passed the defect). A reviewer cell is one letter per
launch -- **B** blocked the stage naming the defect, **W** named it only as a warning, **M**
missed it -- oldest first, so `MBB` is a miss followed by two catches.

A machine-corpus defect never reaches a reviewer, so its reviewer cells are empty by
construction; a reviewer-corpus defect is machine-silent by design, and `calibration/`
says at which revision that silence was last proved.

*Auditor blocks*: machine-silent, and at least one vendor's stage auditor blocked it at least once.
*Red team blocks*: no auditor did, and a red team did. *Nobody blocks*: launched and never blocked
by anyone -- at most warned. *Not measured*: silent, and no reviewer has been shown it.

## What nothing stops

Defects that reached a reviewer and no vendor ever blocked, and machine-silent defects no reviewer
has been shown.

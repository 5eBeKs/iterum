# What the machine does not catch

[Русская версия](GAPS.ru.md)

The machine corpus breaks a demo dataset one mistake at a time and records what every gate said. A mistake no gate catches is never deleted or weakened until it passes: it is recorded here, and the corpus's own test keeps the record true for as long as the gap is open. Of the corpus's 38 mistakes, 1 is such a gap today.
